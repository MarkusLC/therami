Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what data The Rami app (“the app”) collects, what it is used for, where it is stored and how you can delete it. The app is operated by its individual developer, based in Brazil and identified as the seller on the app's App Store page. Contact: corgosinhocorp@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account data. You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We receive from Apple an identifier for your account and, if you allow it, your name and email. If you choose to hide your email, we only receive the relay address created by Apple.
  • App activity. The quests you complete, your progress for the month, your trees, your streak and the date of your last sign-in.
  • Purchases. Which items you bought in the app (trees and prunes) and whether the Rami Plus subscription is active, so we can unlock the content. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive or store card details.

Data we do not collect

The app does not use location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. It does not read data from the Health app: quests in the Health category are just tasks you mark as done. The app has no ads, uses no third-party analytics tools and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use data

We use data only to make the app work: to identify your account, store and sync your progress across devices, unlock what you bought and reply when you ask for support. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who data is shared with

  • Apple. Provides sign-in (Sign in with Apple) and processes purchases and subscriptions through the App Store.
  • Supabase. The database and authentication provider that hosts the app's data, on servers located in Brazil (São Paulo).

Beyond these, we only share data if the law requires it.

Reminders

The daily reminder is scheduled on the device itself. The time you choose is stored only on your iPhone.

How long we keep data

We keep data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and all your progress are erased from our servers. Purchase records kept by Apple are not affected by this deletion.

How to delete your account and data

In the app, open the menu, go to Settings and tap “Delete Account”. Deletion is permanent. It does not cancel your subscription: cancel it first in your iPhone's Settings. If you can't get into the app, request deletion through the contact email.

Your rights

At any time you can ask for confirmation that we process your data, access to it, correction, deletion, portability and information about sharing, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018). If you are in the European Union or anywhere with equivalent rules, you have similar rights under local law. To exercise these rights, write to the contact email. You may also lodge a complaint with Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD) or with the authority in your country.

Security

Data travels over an encrypted connection, and the database access rules let each account read and change only its own data. No system is completely immune to failure, but we treat any incident as a priority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to the contact email so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page, with the update date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: corgosinhocorp@gmail.com.
